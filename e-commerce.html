Project 02

E-Commerce Checkout Optimization

Role: Business Analyst

Focus: Gap analysis

Tools: Excel, user journey mapping

This case study focuses on an online store where many customers abandon their carts at checkout. The project investigates possible reasons for the drop-off and proposes a simpler checkout experience.

What I have done so far

  • Defined the cart abandonment problem.
  • Started a gap analysis between the current checkout and an improved checkout.
  • Outlined customer pain points such as too many steps and unclear payment flow.
  • Prepared recommendations for a shorter and clearer checkout process.

Deliverables

  • Gap analysis report
  • Current vs future checkout process
  • Customer journey notes
  • Improvement proposal
