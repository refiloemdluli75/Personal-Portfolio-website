Project 04

Server Upgrade Risk Register

Role: Business Analyst

Focus: Risk management

Tools: Risk register, Excel

This project looks at a company upgrading its server hardware. The company is worried about data loss, downtime, and service disruption, so the work focuses on identifying and managing those risks.

What I have done so far

  • Defined the main business concern: avoiding downtime and data loss.
  • Listed possible risks that can happen during the upgrade.
  • Planned probability and impact scoring for each risk.
  • Started mitigation ideas such as backups, rollback planning, and communication updates.

Deliverables

  • Risk register
  • Mitigation plan
  • Communication checklist
  • Rollback considerations
